Built by people who know both sides.

NayNay comes from two worlds that don't usually build software together — experienced product and engineering teams who've shipped software people rely on, and horse owners, barn managers, and vets who've lived the chaos this platform fixes.

Our story

Two jobs, one team.

NayNay started with a simple frustration, repeated across every barn we talked to: the information that keeps a horse healthy and a barn running lives everywhere except one place — a phone, a filing cabinet, a text thread, someone's memory. We set out to fix that with people who understood both the software problem and the horse.

Our team pairs software builders with real careers shipping consumer and business products, alongside horse owners, barn managers, and veterinarians who brought the actual workflows — exam documentation, feed schedules, show entries, the handoffs that happen a dozen times a day — into every product decision. NayNay is built by people who've done both jobs: writing the code, and mucking the stall.

Our principles

What shapes what we build.

Built for the barn, not the boardroom

Every feature earns its place by solving something that actually happens in a barn — not by looking good in a pitch deck.

The whole ecosystem matters

Owners, barns, and professionals only benefit from NayNay if all three do. We build for the relationships, not just the individual user.

Care comes first

Every documentation flow, every reminder, every integration exists to help a horse get better care — that's the test every feature has to pass.
