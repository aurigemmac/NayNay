Why integrate

Built for show and race registration platforms.

Every season, riders re-enter the same immunization records and test results into a different registration system. NayNay Connect lets them authorize once and share verified data directly from the source record — so your entry flow gets faster, and the paperwork gets more reliable.

Faster entries

Riders connect their NayNay account instead of hunting down PDFs and re-typing information — fewer abandoned registrations, fewer support tickets about missing documents.

Verified, not re-typed

Horse details, vaccination records, and test results come straight from NayNay's record, not a photo of a form — reducing errors and outdated documentation.

Owner info, ready to go

Contact and ownership details needed for entry paperwork are shared with the rider's authorization, so you're not chasing down who to bill or contact.

The integration

One button. Four data scopes.

NayNay Connect is a drop-in SSO button. Riders sign in with their existing NayNay account and authorize exactly what your platform is requesting — nothing more.

registration.html 
<script src="https://connect.naynay.app/sdk.js" data-client-id="YOUR_CLIENT_ID"></script>

<div id="naynay-connect"
     data-scopes="horse_profile vaccination_records test_results owner_info"
     data-redirect-uri="https://yourdomain.com/naynay/callback">
</div>
badge

horse_profile

Breed, age, sex, color, and registration or pedigree numbers.

vaccines

vaccination_records

Current immunization history, including dates and expirations.

lab_research

test_results

Recent lab and diagnostic results, such as Coggins/EIA testing.

person

owner_info

Name and contact details needed to complete entry paperwork.

Riders see exactly which scopes your platform is requesting before they authorize, and can revoke access from their NayNay account at any time.
